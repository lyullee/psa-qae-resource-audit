# Sanchez 2014 public validation audit

The paper supplies a real bench-scale, high-temperature, multicomponent syngas test with feed, retentate, and permeate GC sampling. Its full mixture contained 17% H2, 19% CO, 7% CO2, and 57% steam; maximum H2 recovery was about 47% at 723 K and 1 MPa. The reported TCD accuracy is 0.01 vol% (about 100 ppm), so the study cannot directly certify the present 0.5 ppm CO or 5 ppm CO2 limits. It is retained as external validation of feasibility, inhibition direction, and recovery scale.
