# CCE main-text figure captions

**Figure 1. Hybrid PSA chance-constrained decision and resource-audit workflow.** The upper path defines the conditional process decision, common uncertainty model, candidate recycle policies, and matched classical/quantum estimators. The lower path separates distribution loading, reversible event evaluation, amplification, QPU execution, and validity checks. Dashed boundaries mark evidence tiers that are not pooled as plant replicates.

**Figure 2. Matched estimator performance for the frozen three-policy PSA event.** Left: high-N reference and replicate summaries for MC, scrambled Sobol RQMC, defensive-mixture IS, and noiseless MLAE under the same correlated prior and purity/recovery event; the horizontal line is the 0.50 feasibility target. Right: probability RMSE across all policies and 200 replications. Local computation times are excluded from the plot because surrogate and likelihood-fit time are not QPU execution time.

**Figure 3. Forward-equivalent crossover and present classical boundary.** Left: the controlled-oracle-plus-loader multiplier at which the ideal MLAE query advantage over equal-RMSE crude MC is lost; the marked spatial/tank proxy is an arithmetic normalization rather than compiled wall time. Right: normalized work for equal-RMSE MC, observed RQMC, and the optimistic MLAE proxy. RQMC has both lower observed RMSE and lower normalized work than MLAE in the primary conditional case.

**Figure 4. Resource ladder from response-event accuracy to calibrated phase-map arithmetic.** (a) Fixed-point response-oracle event-label disagreement and the observed noiseless-MLAE RMSE threshold. (b) Compiled one- and two-bit dense empirical-copula loaders and the unsynthesized seven-bit accuracy tier. (c) Maximum p95 phase-map quantization error normalized by the allocated state/outlet error budgets. (d) Forward and clean compute-uncompute CX counts for 72 reduced-model affine blocks, with callouts for the 20 parameter-only CSS-initializer blocks, 12 event-active primary phase-stream blocks, modular final-event block, and integrated policy stream-to-event oracle. Counts use all-to-all `u`/`cx` compilation and exclude the open resource layers stated in Table 3.

**Figure 5. Real-QPU amplification-noise boundary on `ibm_marrakesh`.** (a) Ideal and observed good-state probabilities for Grover powers 0, 1, and 2 with 4,096 shots per circuit. Error bars show shot uncertainty only. (b) Ideal-model likelihood profile evaluated on the hardware counts; the exact probability 0.625 and the unmitigated maximum-likelihood estimate 0.56694 are separated by hardware-model bias. The ideal likelihood interval is not a noise-calibrated confidence interval.

**Figure 6. Process-model and external-validation boundaries.** (a) Base surrogate probability and the 95% range from resampled leave-one-out residual stress at recycle 0.427. (b) Nominal QAE precision, recovery leave-one-out error, and residual-stress probability half-width. (c) N2-purity response to the three conductance-ratio policies in four scenarios of an independent public CO2/N2 PSA solver. (d) Recovery-policy effects relative to the change induced by fourfold output-grid refinement. The external model is a chemistry analog and does not validate the H2 policy selection.
